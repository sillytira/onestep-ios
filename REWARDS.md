# Personal rewards and rankings — 0.6

Normal, parent, and owner accounts may opt a private planner task into rewards and choose 0–100 points. Private Done works offline immediately. Registration/credit needs the service and retries while the planner is open; no offline balance is invented. The enrollment stays bound to its account UUID across backups and cannot grant login or roles. Children use parent-assigned/approved tasks instead.

The service stores a normalized title hash, reward, IDs, and timestamps for enrolled tasks. The title is transmitted over HTTPS for hashing; private planner content is not otherwise automatically uploaded. Use a 3–160-character title for a reward task. The server waits at least 60 seconds after registration/material edits, spaces counted completions by 30 seconds, allows only one matching normalized title per UTC day, and caps daily counted tasks at 20 and earned points at 500. Registration has a 100/day limit and five-second spacing. Retry/duplicate claims cannot create a second ledger credit. A task beyond a daily cap is recorded without a rank/award, not saved for a later day's credit. Zero-point tasks can count within the task limit.

Weekly top-100 rankings reset their window Monday 00:00 UTC and offer completed-task count or task-earned points. Only opted-in normal/parent/owner profiles appear. Children and linked child identities are excluded. Spending and admin adjustments never inflate earned-point rankings. Self-reported tasks can be gamed within the caps; the filter limits spam rather than proving effort. Parent-approved family awards remain separate and are not included in personal-task rankings.

Screen Time purchases are 15, 30, or 60 minutes. Normal accounts pay one point/minute, up to 120 minutes per UTC day. Linked parents can enable a child policy with 1–10 points/minute and 15–120 daily minutes. The native app shows the policy/cost and submits the expected cost; changed prices are rejected before debit. A durable event ID makes receipt recovery idempotent. Server purchase rules do not grant Apple authorization. See SCREEN-TIME.md.
